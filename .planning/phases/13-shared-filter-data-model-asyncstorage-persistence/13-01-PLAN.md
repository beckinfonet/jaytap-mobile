| T-13-01-03 | Denial of Service | Predicate evaluated O(n) on every render | accept | LOW severity — `properties.filter(buildFilterQuery(...))` is O(n) where n is the home-feed property count (typically <500 for the KG launch market). | T-13-01-04 | Prototype Pollution | `types` array contents | accept | LOW severity — `types` is a string array; the predicate uses `.some(t => t.toLowerCase() === ...)`. No object-spread on untrusted input. No Object.assign of attacker-controlled keys. |
